# Crit 8 reflection

## The breakthrough

At first, my interaction decision for this week was "join with a room
code, no account needed". Then I noticed it was just like Kahoot, which
we use in class. So it was not really my own design. It was just a normal
way to join.

I changed my decision to something that is really about my project: only
the rung that the presenter opens can take cards. In the Ladder of
Feedback, people should go step by step. But in real crits, people often
give suggestions before they even understand the work.

Kahoot still helped me. I kept the quick way to join, but I did not copy
the game parts, like timers, points and a leaderboard. Feedback is not a
competition.

I also asked the agent to deploy early, after step two, not at the end.
This found two bugs that only happened on Fly. Because we found them
early, there was still time to fix them.

## What it changed about me

Now I want to know which parts I borrowed and which parts I designed
myself. Borrowing is fine, but I need to know why I made each choice.
I also learned to test on the real server early, not only on my laptop.
